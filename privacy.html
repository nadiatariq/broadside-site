Privacy Policy

Broadside (iOS app) · Last updated 26 September 2026

In short: Broadside has no servers, no accounts on our side, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything you make and everything you tell the app stays on your iPhone. We never receive it.

What stays on your phone

  • Your designs — posters, their text, photos you add, folders, favourites and trash.
  • Your details (optional) — the name and email you type in "Add your details", or that Apple shares if you use Continue with Apple. They're only used to greet you by name in the app.
  • Your brand kit — brand name, logo, colours and fonts.
  • Settings — appearance, recent searches and similar preferences.
  • Purchase history — a list of what you bought, so the Billing screen can show it.

This information is stored only in the app's storage on your device. It is not uploaded, synced or shared with us or anyone else. It may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, which are controlled by you and Apple.

Photos

Broadside uses Apple's photo picker. The app can only see the specific photos you choose, and only uses them inside your designs.

Sign in with Apple

If you choose Continue with Apple, Apple gives the app an anonymous user identifier and, the first time only, the name and email you choose to share (which can be a private relay address). Broadside keeps these on your phone. You can stop using Apple sign-in for Broadside at any time in Settings → [your name] → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple; the app then removes the details.

Purchases

Print exports and the Pro subscription are paid through Apple's App Store. Apple handles your payment details — we never see them. Apple's own privacy policy applies to those payments. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in your Apple ID settings.

Sharing and exporting

When you share or export a poster, it goes only where you send it (for example Messages, Mail, AirDrop or Files), using iOS's own share sheet.

Removing your information

  • Remove your name and email any time in the app: You → Remove my details.
  • Delete designs from Projects, then delete them from Trash to remove them for good.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone.

Children

Broadside doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
