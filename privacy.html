Privacy Policy

Broadside (iOS app) · Last updated 27 September 2026

In short: Broadside works without an account, and then everything stays on your iPhone. If you sign in and share a design, only that design, its comments and the details needed to collaborate are stored with Google Firebase, our cloud provider. No analytics, no ads, no tracking, and we never sell anything.

What stays on your phone

  • Your designs — posters, their text, photos you add, folders, favourites and trash, until you choose to share one.
  • Your brand kit — brand name, logo, colours and fonts, unless you share it with a Studio team.
  • Settings — appearance, recent searches and similar preferences.
  • Purchase history — a list of what you bought, so the Billing screen can show it.

This information is stored only in the app's storage on your device. It may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, which are controlled by you and Apple.

Signing in (optional)

You only need an account to share links, comment together, invite people or join a team. You can sign in with Apple or with Google. We then store, with Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore:

  • an account identifier created by Firebase;
  • your name and email address (with Sign in with Apple this can be a private relay address);

Other people you collaborate with can see your name and email on shared designs, comments and invitations.

Shared designs, comments and invitations

When you create a share link, invite someone, add a design to a shared folder or join a team, Broadside uploads:

  • that design (its text, layout, photos and a preview picture);
  • comments and replies on it, with who wrote them and when;
  • who it is shared with and their role (view, comment or edit), and invitations you send, including the invitee's email address;
  • for Studio teams, the shared brand kit.

A design shared by link can be seen by anyone who has the link, in the app or on the web at nadiatariq.github.io/broadside-site, until you turn the link off. Designs you have not shared are never uploaded.

Where it is stored

Cloud data is stored by Google Firebase (Google LLC) on servers in India (asia-south1) and processed under Firebase's privacy and security terms. It is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so that only you and the people you share with can read it.

Photos

Broadside uses Apple's photo picker. The app can only see the specific photos you choose, and only uses them inside your designs. A photo is uploaded only if it is part of a design you share.

Purchases

Print exports and subscriptions are paid through Apple's App Store. Apple handles your payment details — we never see them. Apple's own privacy policy applies to those payments. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in your Apple ID settings.

Exporting

When you export a poster or send a file, it goes only where you send it (for example Messages, Mail, AirDrop or Files), using iOS's own share sheet.

Removing your information

  • Turn off a share link or remove people from a design at any time in Share.
  • Delete your account in the app: You → Delete account. This removes your profile and signs you out. Designs you shared that other people still use stay with them; ask us and we will remove them too.
  • Delete designs from Projects, then from Trash, to remove them from your phone.
  • To remove anything else, email us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

Broadside is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
